1.What is Standby mode?
Standby mode is a deep low-power mode of the MCU where:
❌ CPU is off
❌ Most peripherals are off
❌ SRAM and main registers lose their contents
✅ The MCU is still powered
✅ A small “backup power domain” stays alive
*********************************************************************************************************************************************************************************
2.What is tamper detection?
Tamper detection is a hardware security mechanism.
Its job is simple:
	Detect unauthorized physical access or manipulation
	and immediately erase sensitive backup data.
On STM32, that sensitive data usually lives in:
	RTC backup registers
	Sometimes RTC time/date
*********************************************************************************************************************************************************************************
3.What is a tamper pin?
A tamper pin is:
A dedicated GPIO-like pin
Directly connected to the RTC backup domain
Monitored even when the CPU is OFF (Standby mode)
Typical names:
	TAMPER
	TAMP1, TAMP2
	Often multiplexed with an RTC pin
